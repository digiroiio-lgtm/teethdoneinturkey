## Method and limits

- **Overall** = Structure × 0.35 + GEO × 0.4 + Visibility × 0.25. Check weights are in `WEIGHTS` at the top of the script.
- **Structure**: one H1, heading order, title and meta description length, canonical, JSON-LD validity, page-type and breadcrumb schema, FAQ schema matching visible questions, lists/tables, schema dates, word count.
- **GEO**: direct answer near the top, key-facts box, evidence block, visible freshness matching `dateModified`, methodology link, decision support, follow-up question graph, prices consistent with the price index, no unconditional absolute claims.
- **Visibility** uses repo data only: pages linking in from their main content, `public/llms.txt` listing, sitemap lastmod within 90 days, the latest Search Console impressions/position, and GA4 AI-assistant referral sessions (e.g. chatgpt.com) recorded in `seo/serp-history.md` (runs 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-08). These are point-in-time figures from a young property with low volumes; treat them as directional.
- **Not measured**: live Search Console data, rankings for queries not recorded in `serp-history.md`, backlinks, and AI citations that send no click (only referral sessions are recorded). Missing data is excluded from the score, never estimated.
- Price consistency flags a price quoted straight after Straumann, E-max, composite or zirconia that matches no Turkey price in the index; it does not catch every possible wording.
