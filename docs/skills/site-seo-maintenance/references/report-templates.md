# Report templates

## Weekly
Header: period, data sources, data gaps (say "not available" rather than estimating).
Sections: KPI table (this 7d | prev 7d | Δ) · movers by intent cluster · pages changed last week and whether data exists yet · audit errors · recommendations (no edits applied) · deployment-log cross-check.

## Monthly pruning
Status line: "Awaiting approval — nothing applied".
Summary counts per action. Table: `URL | Clicks 90d | Impr 90d | Age | Intent | Duplicate of | Info-gain | Action | Why | Risk`.
Separate sections: local/programmatic (A/B/C), proposed 301s with intent-overlap evidence, sources/trust gaps (missing reviewer, unsourced numbers → verify/qualify/remove).
